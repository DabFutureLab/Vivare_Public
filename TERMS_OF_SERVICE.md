## Appendix B: Skip System

### B.1 What is a Skip?

A "Skip" occurs when:
- You answer "NO" but reflection is invalid
- You submit invalid reflection 7 times
- Day is marked as "Skipped"

### B.2 Skip Consequences

- **7 skips in 30 days:** App locked
- **Lock:** Cannot access reflection
- **Archive:** Still accessible
- **Settings:** Still accessible

### B.3 Unlocking

- **Purchase:** "Remove Lock" ($6.99)
- **Permanent:** One-time purchase
- **Non-refundable:** After use
